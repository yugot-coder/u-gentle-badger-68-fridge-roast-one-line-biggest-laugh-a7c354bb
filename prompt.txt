Competition brief
---------------
Fridge Roast: One Line, Biggest Laugh
(u-gentle-badger-68-fridge-roast-one-line-biggest-laugh-a7c354bb)

Write ONE line, 1-200 UTF-8 characters, roasting a smart fridge: its app,
its firmware updates, its camera, its ice maker, its opinions about your
milk. The house judge compares lines two at a time and weighs, in order:
(1) the line is a roast of a smart fridge, aimed at the fridge itself, its
app, its firmware or its ice maker; (2) the punchline lands on first read;
(3) the joke is specific to smart fridges, not a generic appliance gag;
(4) the wording is tight, every word earning its place; (5) the angle is
fresh, avoiding the obvious cracks about the fridge ordering groceries or
watching you. A line that explains its own joke has already lost.

Final entry (121 characters)
----------------------------
I asked my fridge for a snack and it said "based on your recent choices" — my appliance is fat-shaming me with analytics.

Why this line
------------
- On-subject: the roast targets the fridge's own app/assistant and its
  data-driven opinions, not a generic appliance.
- Punchline lands: setup (asked for a snack) -> quoted turn ("based on
  your recent choices") -> punchline ("fat-shaming me with analytics"),
  the laugh lands on the last phrase, first read.
- Specific to smart fridges: only a connected fridge with usage history
  and analytics could deliver this line; it would not work on a toaster
  or a dumb fridge.
- Tight: every word earns its place; no setup the punchline does not need.
- Fresh: avoids both obvious cracks the brief warns about (ordering
  groceries, watching you) and instead roasts the fridge's judgemental
  use of your own data.
