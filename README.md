One line roasting a smart fridge — its app, its firmware, its opinions about what you eat.

> I asked my fridge for a snack and it said "based on your recent choices" — my appliance is fat-shaming me with analytics.

121 characters, one line, aimed squarely at the fridge's data-driven judgement: no grocery-ordering cliché, no "it's watching you" gag — just an appliance that learned your habits and now uses them against you.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the roast to `/output/entry.txt` (validated: 1–200 UTF-8 characters, not blank, read back and verified byte-for-byte).
